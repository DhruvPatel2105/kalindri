# Competitive Position

Derived from independent competitor research, September 2026. Treat vendor accuracy claims
as marketing unless stated otherwise.

---

## The headline finding

**No competitor delivers a dynamic blocking-skill diagnosis.** Not one platform reviewed
computes, from a user's own current scores, which single skill is capping their CLB under
IRCC's lowest-skill rule, and tells them the exact point gap.

The differentiator holds. With one caveat below.

---

## The one real threat: ptecorepractice.com

Small, independent, GitHub-hosted, Core-only, explicitly targeting Canada PR and CLB. Around
1,500 questions, bilingual English/Chinese.

It is the only platform that already has:

- a per-skill CLB calculator
- explicit editorial guidance to "always prioritize the lowest-skill score"
- articulated cross-skill logic: "High-impact integrated tasks like Repeat Sentence and
  Write From Dictation create cross-skill gains"

**But its own pricing table lists the "Personalized CLB Study Plan" as a "Static target
plan."** It is editorial content, not a per-user engine. They understand the insight and
have not productised it.

Pricing: free tier 10 questions per module per day; premium $14.99 CAD / 7 days,
$28.99 / 30 days, $48.99 / 90 days. No auto-renew, no refunds.

**Implication:** they are one product iteration from occupying your position. Execution
speed matters. Ship the dynamic version — recomputed each session, quantified in points to
the next CLB, updating as scores change — before they do.

## Pearson's own products are your calibration source

**Scored Practice Test — around USD $35.99.** Uses "real past test questions and the same
scoring algorithm as the PTE Core test." Designed to be taken once, valid one year. Gives
an overall and per-skill breakdown.

This is the only product on the market using the genuine KAT/LSA/Versant engine. Buying 3–5
and having staging students sit them produces response-and-real-score pairs that no
competitor possesses.

**Question Bank — 300 practice questions with official model answers**, bundled with the
Official Guide and a preparation course. A legitimately licensed content source, worth
weighing against the provenance risk of the institute's bank. Note that Question Bank tasks
are not scored — they provide sample answers only.

## Complaints as a feature list

Ordered by recurrence. This is what to build in order to beat them.

### 1. Speaking scoring that can be trusted (most cited, most damaging)

APEUni is widely reported to under-score speaking, especially fluency and Describe Image.
Documented cases: students scoring low 50s on APEUni then 80+ on the real test; another
scoring 50–60 then full marks. The structural critique is the damaging one — **"it
discourages Fluency because you have to read one word at a time to get decent scores."**
The platform actively teaches a habit that hurts real performance.

AlfaPTE users report speaking responses that simply never score, across multiple devices and
microphones.

**Build:** a speaking scorer validated against real outcomes, that explicitly rewards
natural connected speech over robotic delivery, and that never silently fails to score.

### 2. Accuracy you can prove

Every rival claims a number with no methodology, correlation study, or sample published.
Publishing a real validation against actual Pearson results would be unique in this market
and is achievable with a handful of Scored Practice Tests.

### 3. Error-specific feedback

Users describe feedback as "vague" and results as "unclear," including on platforms that
market detailed analysis. Name the exact error in the student's own response — the
unaddressed bullet point, the mispronounced word, the specific grammar position — rather
than boilerplate.

### 4. Question-bank integrity

Documented complaints include mismatched text and audio, missing Writing sections, and
outdated formats. Core-correct items with the right word limits and trait maxima are
themselves a differentiator, given how many platforms bolt Core onto an Academic product.

### 5. Never lose an attempt

Blank-screen app failures, speaking jobs that never score, single-attempt mocks that cannot
be retaken. Robust recording, retry, and crash-resume are competitive features here, not
hygiene.

### 6. Fair commercial terms

Gurully's zero-refund policy, account deactivation following a negative review, and a
documented request that a reviewer delete their review in place of a refund. Transparent
terms and no auto-renew traps are a marketing point, not just ethics.

## Positioning

**Lead with the blocking-skill diagnosis.** Dynamic, recomputed each session, quantified in
points to the next CLB, framed around IRCC's lowest-skill rule. This is the headline, above
any score.

**Second pillar: the cross-skill recommender.** Map all 19 task types to the skills they
feed, then recommend the practice mix that lifts the blocking skill fastest per unit of
time. Nobody has this.

**Third: win trust on speaking and publish the validation.** This attacks APEUni's largest
weakness and every rival's unsubstantiated claim simultaneously.

**Fourth: Core-correct content and error-level feedback from day one.**

---

## What would change the plan

- **ptecorepractice.com ships a dynamic blocking-skill engine** → accelerate; out-execute on
  validation and the recommender
- **Pearson extends Official AI Practice with CLB-blocker guidance** → pivot to price, UX and
  error-specificity. You cannot out-calibrate the real engine.
