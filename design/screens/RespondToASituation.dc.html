Respond to a Situation Speaking · Practice

You will hear a description of a situation. You will have 20 seconds to think about your answer, then 40 seconds to speak. You will hear the description only once.

You share a driveway with your neighbour. After the last snowfall they cleared only their own half, and the ridge of snow left between the two halves now blocks your car. You have a good relationship with them and want to keep it. What would you say to your neighbour?

Scored on appropriateness, oral fluency and pronunciation. Speak to the person, not about them — an answer that describes the situation instead of addressing your neighbour loses appropriateness marks.
