Kalindri · Exam components

Nine components, nineteen question types

Every screen is built from the components below. Same positions and behaviour as the real exam; teal-on-warm-paper palette so it reads as Kalindri. Everything here is live — the timers tick, the countdown rolls into Recording, the word banks and transcripts respond.

StatusBox Appears whenever audio or video plays. Bordered white card, status line and playback progress. No volume — that lives on its own.
